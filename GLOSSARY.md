# Neiro UI

The client interface and plugin composition domain for Neiro across desktop, tablet, and foldable mobile devices.

## Language

**Shell** (`美学形态壳层`):
An indivisible visual and spatial paradigm plugin that owns workspace grid topology, typography, geometric language, navigation, and signature audio interactions across viewports.
_Avoid_: 皮肤, 主题, 布局模板, Surface

**Acoustic FX** (`声学视觉层`):
The audio-reactive GPU visual intensity (`Off`, `Pulse`, `Canvas`) and physical tactile foley feedback driven by live playback telemetry.
_Avoid_: 特效, 背景光, 动画开关

**Custom Shell** (`特型壳层`):
An independent third-party or experimental `ui.shell` plugin loaded outside the standard built-in Shell catalog.
_Avoid_: 全屏皮肤, 外部主题

**Host Core** (`宿主内核`):
The immutable safety foundation present beneath all shells, owning audio engine connectivity, permission gates, core modal sheets, and safe-mode recovery.
_Avoid_: 主程序, 壳层宿主

**Hexahedron** (`六面体硬件壳层`):
The spatial 6-face physical hardware Shell paradigm modeled after modular broadcast audio instruments (`BC-1` + `AMP-35`), where each face owns one primary audio domain and cold-launches in a `3/4` hero perspective.
_Avoid_: 3D皮肤, 立方体皮肤

**Album Dossier** (`专辑实体档案`):
The foreground physical unfold triggered from the Front CD Magazine, opening an album as a kraft sleeve, loadable disc, and printed tracklist booklet without flipping away from the cube.
_Avoid_: 专辑弹窗, 详情卡片

**Grille Spectrum** (`网罩矩阵频谱`):
The backlit LED matrix spectrum analyzer embedded behind the Right Face's perforated speaker grille, visualizing live FFT energy directly through the physical chassis perforations.
_Avoid_: 装饰网孔, 假频谱图
